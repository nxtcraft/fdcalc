What is a Fixed Deposit?

A Fixed Deposit (FD) is a financial instrument offered by banks and NBFCs where you deposit a lump sum for a fixed tenure at a predetermined interest rate. Unlike savings accounts, the rate is locked in for the full tenure regardless of changes in market interest rates, giving you guaranteed, predictable returns.

Key characteristics of FDs in India:

  • DICGC insured up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank — principal + interest combined. This makes bank FDs among the safest investments available in India.
  • Tenure ranges from 7 days to 10 years. Rates vary by tenure — longer is not always higher.
  • Can be opened for individuals, HUFs, senior citizens, NRIs (NRE/NRO FDs), and companies.
  • Premature withdrawal is allowed in most cases, with a penalty of 0.5%–1%.
  • Interest can be paid monthly, quarterly, or at maturity depending on your preference.

FD Interest Calculation Formula

Most bank FDs in India use quarterly compounding. The maturity amount is calculated as:

Maturity = P × (1 + r/n)n×t

Where: P = Principal (₹), r = Annual interest rate as decimal (e.g. 7% → 0.07), n = Compounding frequency per year (4 for quarterly), t = Tenure in years.

Worked example — ₹1,00,000 at 7% for 3 years (quarterly compounding):

Maturity = 1,00,000 × (1 + 0.07/4)4×3 = 1,00,000 × (1.0175)12 = ₹1,23,144

Interest earned = ₹23,144

Tax on FD Interest

FD interest is fully taxable in India. Here's exactly how it works:

  • TDS (Tax Deducted at Source): Banks deduct TDS at 10% when your total interest from that bank in a financial year exceeds ₹40,000 (₹50,000 for senior citizens). TDS is deducted even on interest accrued but not yet paid (for cumulative FDs).
  • Actual tax: Interest is added to your total income and taxed at your slab rate. If you're in the 30% slab, TDS covers only 10% — you owe the remaining 20% at ITR filing.
  • No PAN = higher TDS: If you haven't submitted PAN, TDS is deducted at 20% instead of 10%.
  • Form 15G / 15H: Submit at the start of the financial year to request no TDS deduction if your total income is below the taxable limit. 15G for below 60 years, 15H for 60+.

Post-tax effective yield example: ₹1 lakh at 7.5% (₹7,500 interest) in the 20% slab → actual tax = ₹1,500 → effective post-tax yield = 6.0%. The FD calculator's tax field shows this automatically.

Tax-saver FD (5-year FD under Section 80C): You get an 80C deduction of up to ₹1.5 lakh on the principal invested. However, the interest is fully taxable. These FDs have a 5-year lock-in; premature withdrawal is not allowed.

Senior Citizen FD: Special Benefits

All banks offer senior citizens (60+) an additional 0.25%–0.75% interest rate over and above the regular FD rate. At ₹1 lakh and 7.5% (vs 7%), this extra 0.5% earns ₹500 more per year — small but adds up across a larger retirement corpus.

  • Higher TDS threshold: ₹50,000 (vs ₹40,000 for general public) before TDS kicks in.
  • Senior Citizens' Saving Scheme (SCSS): Currently 8.2% pa, government-backed, max ₹30 lakh investment, 5-year tenure extendable by 3 years. Better than most bank FDs for senior citizens.
  • Post Office Monthly Income Scheme (POMIS): 7.4% with monthly interest payout, max ₹9L (single) / ₹15L (joint). Good for regular income.
  • Form 15H: Senior citizens can avoid TDS completely if total income is below ₹3L (old regime) or ₹7.5L (new regime after standard deduction).

Frequently Asked Questions

How is FD interest calculated in India?

Maturity = P × (1 + r/n)^(n×t) for compound interest (most bank FDs). P = principal, r = annual rate as decimal, n = compounding periods/year (4 for quarterly), t = tenure in years. For simple interest: Maturity = P + P×r×t. Example: ₹1L at 7% quarterly for 1 year = ₹1,07,186.

Is FD safe in India?

Bank FDs are very safe — deposits are insured by DICGC (a subsidiary of RBI) up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank (principal + interest combined). For amounts above ₹5L, spread across multiple banks. Post Office FDs are backed directly by the Government of India.

Can I break an FD early?

Yes. Premature closure is allowed at most banks with a penalty of 0.5%–1%. The bank pays interest at the rate applicable for the period actually held, minus the penalty. Some banks offer penalty-free premature withdrawal on specific products. Loan against FD (up to 90% of FD value) is a better option if you need funds temporarily.

Which bank has the highest FD rate in India 2024-25?

Small finance banks (ESAF, Unity, Suryoday, Jana, UJJIVAN) offer 8–9% for general and 8.5–9.5% for senior citizens. DICGC insures ₹5L per bank. Major banks (SBI 6.5–7.1%, HDFC 7–7.4%, ICICI 6.7–7.25%) are safer for larger amounts. Use the FD calculator to compare effective maturity across different rates.

Is 5-year FD tax-free?

The 5-year tax-saving FD gives you an 80C deduction (up to ₹1.5L) on the principal — not tax-free returns. The interest earned is fully taxable at your slab rate. Lock-in is 5 years, no premature withdrawal allowed. If you're in the 30% slab, the tax-saving FD's post-tax effective yield is similar to a regular short-term FD.
